ze-ads-engine – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

ze-ads-engine is an internal advertising-management tool operated by Prure Group (Meta business portfolio "PG Dental Tourism Tracking"), an advertising agency based in Tirana, Albania. It is used only by the agency's own staff to manage Meta ad accounts that the agency owns or that its clients have shared with it. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside the agency logs in to it.

1. What the app does

  • Reads campaign, ad set, ad and performance data (spend, impressions, clicks, results) from the Meta Marketing API for the ad accounts the agency manages.
  • Prepares changes (pausing ads, adjusting budgets, creating new ads that start paused) that a member of the agency approves before they are applied.
  • Produces internal reports for the agency and its clients.

2. Data we receive from Meta

  • Advertising data: ad account, campaign, ad set, ad and creative settings, and aggregated performance metrics. These are business data, not data about individual people.
  • Agency staff: the Meta user ID and name of the agency staff member whose access token the app uses, and the list of Pages and ad accounts that person manages.

The app does not read the contents of lead forms, does not read messages, and does not receive personal data of people who see or respond to ads. It does not use Facebook Login for the public.

3. How we use the data

Only to manage and report on the advertising of the agency and its clients. We do not sell the data, do not use it to build profiles of individuals, and do not share it with third parties other than the client who owns the ad account concerned.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the agency's own workstation in Albania, in storage that only agency staff can access. Access tokens are kept in a protected configuration file and are never shared. Data is kept for as long as the agency manages the ad account, and for at most 24 months after that.

5. Your rights and data deletion

If you are an agency staff member or a client whose ad account is managed with this app, you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you or your ad account. To request deletion, send an email to andreasprure@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – ze-ads-engine" and the ad account or Meta user concerned. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also remove the app's access at any time in your Facebook settings under "Business integrations".
